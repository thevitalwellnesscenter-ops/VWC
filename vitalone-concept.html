Product concept · The Vital Wellness Center

VitalOne

A cloud EHR for primary care that pairs the clinical depth, revenue cycle, and patient engagement of athenaOne with the workflow speed and scheduling flexibility of the Opti family — one chart, one bill, one front door for the patient.

Inherited from athenaOne Inherited from Opti New in VitalOne

Every feature below is tagged with its lineage, so you can see exactly what "best of both" means in practice.

01 · The synthesis

What each parent contributes

athenaOne is the insurance-grade backbone; Opti (OptiMantra's scheduling and billing flexibility, EmOpti's throughput mindset) is the agility layer. VitalOne is not a bundle of the two — it is one system designed around what each does best.

From athenaOne

Depth & compliance

  • athenaClinicals-style charting — structured encounter notes, order sets, lab and imaging integration, longitudinal patient history.
  • athenaCollector-style revenue cycle — payer rules engine, claim scrubbing before submission, denial management, eligibility checks.
  • athenaCommunicator-style engagement — automated reminders, patient portal, self-service intake and messaging.
  • Network-scale intelligence: rules learned across practices reduce claim rejections for everyone.
From Opti

Speed & flexibility

  • OptiMantra-style scheduling — per-provider visit types, rooms and equipment as bookable resources, packages and memberships.
  • Hybrid payment models — cash, insurance, superbills, and subscription billing living side by side on one invoice.
  • EmOpti-style throughput — a live queue view of the clinic day, rapid triage-to-room workflows, built-in telehealth handoff.
  • Configurable by the practice, not by a vendor services team.

02 · Core modules

Four modules, one record

Each module reads and writes the same patient record and the same ledger. Nothing is synced, because nothing is separate.

Clinical Charting

VO-CLIN

The chart a primary care physician actually works in all day: encounter documentation, problem lists, medications, orders, and results — with AI-drafted notes the clinician edits rather than writes.

  • athenaOneStructured SOAP encounters with specialty templates, order sets, and e-prescribing (EPCS-ready).
  • athenaOneLab & imaging loop — orders out, discrete results back into the chart with flags and trending.
  • OptiFlexible note types — quick visits, nurse-only encounters, and wellness protocols without full-note overhead.
  • VitalOneAmbient AI scribe — drafts the note from the visit conversation; clinician signs, never transcribes.

Billing & Revenue

VO-REV

Insurance-grade revenue cycle that also treats cash, packages, and memberships as first-class citizens — because a modern primary care practice runs on both.

  • athenaOneClaim scrubbing & payer rules — errors caught before submission; denials worked from a single queue.
  • athenaOneReal-time eligibility and patient responsibility estimates at check-in, not at statement time.
  • OptiCash, packages, memberships — recurring billing, prepaid visit packs, and superbill generation.
  • VitalOneOne ledger — insurance and self-pay charges reconcile on a single patient balance and a single day-sheet.

Scheduling & Patient Engagement

VO-FLOW

The front door and the daily rhythm of the clinic: booking, reminders, intake, messaging, and a live view of where every patient is in their visit.

  • athenaOneAutomated outreach — reminders, recalls, and no-show follow-up by text, email, and voice.
  • athenaOnePatient portal — results, messaging, bill pay, and self-scheduling from one login.
  • OptiResource-aware scheduling — providers, rooms, and equipment booked together; waitlist auto-fills cancellations.
  • OptiLive clinic queue — arrival to room to checkout on one board, with telehealth visits in the same lanes.

Analytics

VO-INSIGHT

Practice performance without exports: clinical quality, financial health, and operational throughput measured continuously from the same records the modules above create.

  • athenaOneRevenue cycle dashboards — days in A/R, clean-claim rate, denial reasons, payer mix.
  • athenaOneQuality measure tracking — MIPS/HEDIS-style gap lists with patient-level drill-down.
  • OptiThroughput metrics — wait time, cycle time, room utilization, no-show rate by visit type.
  • VitalOneMorning huddle brief — an auto-generated one-pager of today's schedule risks, care gaps, and open balances.

04 · Roadmap

Path to first patient

1

Foundation

Months 0–6

Patient record, scheduling with resource booking, basic charting, and cash/package billing. A wellness-forward practice can run its whole day on VitalOne — before insurance is in the picture.

2

Insurance-grade

Months 6–14

Eligibility, claim scrubbing, clearinghouse submission, denial management, and the unified ledger. e-Prescribing and lab interfaces land here. This is the athenaCollector-parity milestone.

3

Intelligence

Months 14–24

Ambient AI scribe, live clinic queue with throughput analytics, quality measure tracking, and the morning huddle brief. The system stops recording the practice and starts running it.
